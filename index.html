02 — About

The
Story

A designer obsessed with the spaces between things — negative space, quiet moments, the pause before the word.

With years of practice across visual identity, editorial photography, and art direction, the work lives at the intersection of restraint and expression. Every project begins with listening — to the brief, to the image, to the silence that tells you where to go next.

Based between studios and open skies, the practice spans brand identities, motion pieces, and long-form photographic essays that question how we see the everyday.
